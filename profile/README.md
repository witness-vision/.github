  ## Witness                                                                                                                                  
  
  **Consumer deepfake detection.** Drop in any image. Get a clear answer: real or synthetic, with a confidence score and a plain-language     
  explanation. In seconds.                                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                              
  Witness checks content against an ensemble of research-backed detection models from peer-reviewed papers at top computer vision conferences.
  Confidence scores, not certainties. Honest about limits.
                                                                                                                                              
  ### Surfaces                                                                                                                                
  
  - **Chrome extension** — scans images and video directly on any page you browse                                                             
  - **Web app** — drag, paste, or upload at [witness.vision](https://witness.vision)                                                        
  - **Mobile app** — coming soon                                                                                                              
  - **API & SDKs** — coming soon                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                              
  ### How it works                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                              
  1. You drop in an image or video.                                                                                                           
  2. Multiple detection models analyze it in parallel.
  3. You get a clear verdict: real or synthetic, with a confidence score.
